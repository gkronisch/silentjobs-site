Job searching shouldn't feel like a second job

📧

Inbox Chaos

Dozens of alert emails a day from different job boards, and only a few are worth opening.

🎯

Weak Filters

Most alert filters can't express "remote, or hybrid near me" or "no contract roles," so irrelevant jobs keep coming.

🔁

Same Jobs Everywhere

The same opening shows up on several sites and in several emails, so you read it again and again.

⏰

Time Drain

Checking each board, skipping duplicates and skimming postings that don't fit adds up to hours every week.

What SilentJobs Will Do

We're building it now. Here's what it's designed to do.

🎯

Your Criteria, Strictly Applied

Set job titles, locations, remote or hybrid, a salary floor and keywords to exclude. Jobs that don't fit stay out of your digest.

You set the rules
📅

One Daily Digest

Every match in a single email, at the time of day you choose.

One email a day
🌐

One List, Many Sources

Openings from more than one job board gathered into one list, so you don't have to juggle a dozen separate alerts.

Sources added over time
🚫

Each Job Once

When the same role is posted on several sites, you see it once.

No repeats
🔔

Only What's New

Each digest includes only jobs posted since the last one, so you're not reading about roles that closed weeks ago.

New since your last digest

Your Data Stays Yours

Your search preferences are used to build your digest and nothing else. We don't sell your data.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

SilentJobs is a job search product in development. This policy covers this website (silentjobs.app) and its waitlist.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your name,
  • your email address, and
  • if you choose to share it, the role you're looking for.

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

As with any website, our hosting provider and Google Fonts (which serves the site's fonts) receive your IP address and basic browser details when you load a page. Their own privacy policies cover how they handle that.

How we use it

We use your information only to tell you about SilentJobs early access and, if you reply to us, to talk with you about what you need from it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or share your information. Waitlist signups are received and stored for us by Formspree, the form service we use. See Formspree's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep waitlist information until you ask us to remove it or we close the waitlist. Email support@silentjobs.app to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to leave the waitlist. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe.

Do Not Track

This site doesn't track you across other websites, so it doesn't change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals.

Children

SilentJobs isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll email you before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@silentjobs.app
